Project information

What information is stored?

When you create an account, MediAid stores the email address, username, and a password hash needed to provide sign-in. Contact-form submissions are stored in the application database with the name, email address, subject, message, and submission time.

How is your password handled?

Passwords are stored as one-way hashes rather than readable text. Never reuse a password you use for another service.

What should I avoid sharing?

Do not send medical records, symptoms requiring urgent attention, financial details, or other sensitive personal information through the contact form. MediAid is not an emergency or clinical service.

Who can access this information?

Information is stored in the project’s configured database. The application does not currently provide a public data export or deletion tool. Contact the project administrator to ask about account or message data.

Important

This page describes the current project behavior and is not legal advice or a complete privacy notice for a production healthcare service.
