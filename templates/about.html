ABOUT MEDIAID

Healthcare information, made easier to navigate.

Finding the right place to start can feel overwhelming. MediAid brings helpful care-finding tools together in one straightforward place.

Our purpose

MediAid is a project designed to make it easier to explore healthcare information, including hospital and doctor directories and general medical guidance.

The goal is to help people find a useful next step—not to diagnose a condition, replace a clinician, or guarantee that a provider listing is current.
