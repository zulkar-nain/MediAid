URGENT CARE

If you need emergency help, act now.

MediAid is not an emergency service. Do not wait for a response from this website.

Contact emergency services now

Call the emergency number for your location or go to the nearest emergency department. If you cannot make the call, ask someone nearby to contact emergency services for you.

Emergency numbers and hospital contacts vary by location. Use a number you can verify locally; this page does not provide unverified contact details.

When speaking with emergency services

  • Give your exact location and a callback number.
  • Briefly describe what is happening and when it started.
  • Follow the emergency operator’s instructions.

This information is not a substitute for emergency services or professional medical advice.
